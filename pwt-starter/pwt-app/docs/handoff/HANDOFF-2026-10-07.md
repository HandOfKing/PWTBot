## 2. What the first full-match run showed (2026-10-04 22-55-47, 24 fps, app 0.1.0)

Its end-of-match board is `Video Project 11` (truth: `tests/fixtures/match_board_vp11.csv`).
- **Board:** 12/12 players, 58/60 numbers, 0 wrong. Board total 171 deaths.
- **Feed:** 219 eliminations, 100 named, 43 NO_FEED_ROW, 22 rounds of 25.

Causes found, all fixed in `31beafb` (app 0.2.0, schema v3):
1. **Name junk.** The engine matched against every player in the DB: 134 names, 119 of them misreads from
   an Oct 4 16:38 run of the old CLI with the 2v2 profile on the Oct 1 file. The app pre-filled the names
   box from that table. The roster also lacked Anoydyne15op, DeathwishツSpy and BruceWayne³.
   - Fix: match only the run's roster plus aliases (`db.roster_names`). The box fills from the last-used
     list (`roster.txt` in the data folder), else the bundled one. Off-roster board names are reported,
     never used for the feed.
   - Measured on the run's 385 evidence crops (one Tesseract read each): 179 -> 312 rows with both names.
     16/16 random new ones checked by eye.
2. **Rounds lost after blue reached 10.** The banner test sampled pixels inside the score box; the "0" of
   "10" covers (805,110). Confirmed on `Video Project 13`.
   - Fix: share of each score box team-coloured >= 0.25. One digit gives 0.61-0.83, "10" gives 0.38-0.40,
     hidden gives 0.00.
   - Backstops: a Remaining reset after a board starts the next round on its first death (or on a feed row
     >= 8 s after the reset); a short board for an unseen round creates it.
   - A board up >= 5 s, or still up at the end, is the match board. Later match boards replace earlier ones.
3. **Remaining 5 and 8 unreadable**, plus "11" when the two 1s touch, so final drops were lost.
   - Harvested from VP13 (`tools/harvest_remaining_digits.py`, truth `tests/fixtures/remaining_vp13.csv`):
     0 WRONG, held out 2664/2701.
4. **Late rows of a wipe.** The feed prints them ~2.5 s apart; some appear after the board, in the next
   round. They are now assigned to the previous round:
   - within 8 s of a round start;
   - kill rows within 15 s while the round has no death yet.
   - They may claim any unclaimed death of that round. At the end, leftover kill rows claim leftover deaths
     of their round. A kill row with no death is flagged `NO_DROP` and not counted.
5. **Summary sheet** compared against summed round boards (6/12 rows each). It now uses the match board;
   the Check column covers elims and knocks vs Knock Outs.

Schema v3 deletes `pipeline_version LIKE '0.1%'` matches and their evidence, then orphan players, on first open.
